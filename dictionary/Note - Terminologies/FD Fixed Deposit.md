Fixed deposit = Khoản tiết kiệm cố định (7 days to 10 years)


#### Interest Rates:
- **Fixed Deposits**: Typically offer higher interest rates, ranging from 5% to 8%, depending on the bank and tenure. The interest rate is fixed for the duration of the deposit. 

- **Savings Accounts**: Generally provide lower interest rates, usually between 2% to 4%. The rates can vary and are often subject to change based on market conditions. 


#### Liquidity:
- **Fixed Deposits**: Funds are locked in for a specified period (ranging from 7 days to 10 years), and early withdrawals may incur penalties. 

- **Savings Accounts**: Allow for immediate access to funds without penalties, making them ideal for everyday transactions and emergencies.
