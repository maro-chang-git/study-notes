### Permanent Blacklist Criteria

Customers will be permanently blacklisted if they meet any of the following conditions:

- Labeled as Bad Bank.
- Have Written-off or sold debt within the last 5 years.
- Have been classified as NPL within the last 5 years.

### Temporary Blacklist (Applied for 2 Years)

Customers will be blacklisted for 2 years if they fall under any of the following categories:

- Had 10 days past due (10+ DPD) in the last 3 months
- Had 30+ DPD in the last 6 months
- Had 60+ DPD in the last 9 months
- Had 90+ DPD in the last 12 months

### Note
- Bad Bank: applied to customers who are considered extremely high-risk or problematic by the financial institution.

- Written-off: the lender (e.g., a bank) has determined that t**he debt is unlikely to be collected and has removed** it from its active accounts receivable. This typically happens when the borrower has defaulted and recovery efforts have failed.

- NPL: Non-Performing Loan - the borrower **has not made scheduled payments**(principal or interest) for a prolonged period, typically 90 days or more past due.
