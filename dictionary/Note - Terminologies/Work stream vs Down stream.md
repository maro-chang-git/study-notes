### ✅ Workstream

A workstream is essentially a **logical grouping of activities or tasks within a project or program.**
In banking IT, workstreams are often used in large transformation programs (e.g., core banking migration, digital banking implementation).
Each workstream focuses on a specific domain or function, such as:

- Payments Workstream – handling payment systems integration.
- Core Banking Workstream – managing core banking platform changes.
- Compliance Workstream – ensuring regulatory requirements.

**Purpose:** Helps organize responsibilities, timelines, and deliverables across multiple teams.

### ✅ Downstream

Downstream refers to systems or **processes that receive data or outputs from another system.**
In banking IT architecture:

If Core Banking System sends transaction data to Risk Management System, then Risk Management is downstream of Core Banking.

**Upstream vs Downstream:**
- Upstream: The source system providing data.
- Downstream: The receiving system consuming that data.

**Example:**
- Upstream: Customer onboarding system.
- Downstream: CRM, AML (Anti-Money Laundering), and reporting systems.

**How They Relate**
- Workstream is about project organization.
- Downstream is about data flow and system dependencies.
