KYC = Know Your customer
It's a compliance process used by banks and financial institutions to **verify customer identities.**

### Key Components


1. Customer Identification
Collecting and verifying personal details such as:
- Full name
- Date of birth
- Address
- Government-issued ID (passport, national ID, driver’s license)

2. Customer Due Diligence (CDD)
Assessing the risk level of the customer based on their profile and transactions.

3. Ongoing Monitoring
Continuously monitoring transactions to detect suspicious activities.


### In Digital Banking

- eKYC (Electronic KYC): Uses digital methods like:

Document upload and verification
Biometric authentication (fingerprint, facial recognition)
Video KYC (live video verification)

- Automation & AI: Speeds up verification and reduces manual errors.
