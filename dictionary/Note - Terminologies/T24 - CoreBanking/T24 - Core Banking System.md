### **What T24 Is**

**Temenos T24 (now often called Temenos Transact)** is a **Core Banking System (CBS)** — a comprehensive platform that manages a bank’s daily operations, including:

- Customer accounts
- Deposits and loans
- Payments and transfers
- Treasury operations
- Risk management
- Regulatory reporting

It provides the **centralized engine** that processes transactions and maintains customer records across all branches and channels.

---

### ⚙️ **Key Features**

1. **Modular Architecture** – Banks can implement only the modules they need (e.g., Retail Banking, Corporate Banking, Treasury).
    
2. **Real-time Processing** – Transactions are processed instantly, ensuring up-to-date balances and reports.
    
3. **Multi-currency & Multi-country Support** – Ideal for international banks.
    
4. **APIs & Integration** – Works with digital channels (mobile apps, online banking) and fintech solutions.
    
5. **Cloud & On-Premise Deployment** – Can be deployed on private servers or cloud platforms like AWS, Azure, or Temenos’ own cloud.

- **Modular architecture**: Banks can implement only the modules they need.
- **Open standards**: Runs on open hardware, databases, and supports integration via APIs and web services.
- **Scalability**: Supports growth across geographies and business lines.
- **Customization**: Can be tailored to specific banking needs using tools like TAFJ (Temenos Application Framework) and TPH (Temenos Process Handler). [[sumble.com]](https://sumble.com/tech/t24)
